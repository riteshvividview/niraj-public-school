Page 01 Feature catalogue

What the platform includes

Each school runs on its own dedicated workspace, listing exactly what a student receives — books, uniform, kit and stationery — plus programs and events. Payment happens online; a digital receipt is generated and shown at the school to collect items or attend the program. Nothing is delivered.

Accounts & School Workspace

Every school has its own dedicated workspace — a parent or student account is linked directly to their assigned school, with no cross-school browsing.

Login & Logout

  • Mobile number / OTP based account login
  • Secure logout and session handling
  • Password-free, simple for non-technical users

Single Parent/Student Profile

  • One straightforward profile per user — no family or multi-child switching
  • Shows their own purchases, programs and receipts

Dedicated School Workspace

  • Each school is assigned its own workspace on the platform
  • A user's account is tied to their one assigned school
  • No search or discovery of other schools' essentials

Multilingual Interface

  • Full app available in major Indian languages
  • Language preference saved per profile
  • Consistent translation across every screen

Essentials Store

Books, uniform & kit, and stationery — exactly what the school has listed for that student. Nothing is picked à la carte; everything is paid for and collected in person.

Book List

  • Class and board-wise list published by the school
  • Student sees exactly which books they get — not a choice, a fixed list
  • Pay online for the full set, then show the receipt to collect it at school

Uniform & Kit Store

  • Uniform sets, PT kit and house-colour items listed by size
  • Size guide to help confirm the correct fit before paying
  • Pay online, then collect the confirmed kit at school

Stationery & Essentials

  • Bags, notebooks, geometry box and other essentials the school lists
  • Shown as the fixed starter-kit set for that class
  • Added to the same cart as books and uniform

Programs & Events

The one part of the platform that works across every school — a single global tab where any school can post a program, and any user can find and pay for it, regardless of their assigned school.

Global Programs Feed

  • Workshops, sports day, annual day, camps, extra classes
  • Posted by any school, visible across the whole platform
  • Filter by school, category, date or fee

Program Details & Seats

  • Date, venue, description and fee for each program
  • Seat availability shown before payment
  • Organising school's contact info displayed

Pay & Get Ticket

  • Pay the program fee online in the same cart flow
  • Digital ticket/receipt with QR code generated instantly
  • Shown at entry for the school to verify and admit

Payments & Receipts

The core mechanic of the platform — pay online, get a receipt, redeem it in person.

Unified Cart & Checkout

  • Books, uniform, stationery and program fees in one cart
  • UPI, cards and net banking supported
  • No admission fee or delivery charge involved

Digital Receipt with QR Code

  • Generated instantly after successful payment
  • Shown to the school to collect items or enter a program
  • Downloadable and shareable as PDF or image

Receipt History

  • Every past payment and receipt kept in one place
  • Organised by date and by school
  • Useful as proof if a receipt needs to be reprinted

School Console

What a school needs to publish its own workspace listings and verify payments on the ground.

Catalogue Manager

  • Publish the fixed book, uniform and stationery list per class
  • Update prices, sizes and availability anytime

Program Manager

  • Create and publish events with date, fee and seat count
  • Close registration once seats are filled

Receipt / QR Verification

  • Scan a user's QR receipt at the counter or event gate
  • Marks the item collected or the attendee checked in

Payment & Redemption Reports

  • See who has paid versus who has actually collected/attended
  • Export summaries for accounting

Platform Foundation

The backend that makes accounts, payments and receipts reliable.

Central Database

  • Stores schools, catalogues, programs, accounts and receipts
  • One source of truth across every school workspace

Secure Login System

  • OTP-based authentication and session management
  • Role-based access for parents, schools and platform admins

Notifications

  • Payment success and receipt-ready alerts
  • Reminders for upcoming programs/events

Help & Support

  • Multilingual chat/help for payment or receipt issues
  • FAQ for common checkout and collection questions

Page 03 Research-backed additions

What else could be added

Based on how established school-payment platforms and Indian EdTech/data-privacy practice handle this exact model, here is what's worth adding once the core platform above is validated. These are proposed additions, not committed scope.

Engagement & Payments

What keeps users coming back and reduces missed payments — seen across mainstream school-payment platforms.

Suggested

Automated Payment Reminders

  • SMS / WhatsApp / push reminders before a program's registration closes
  • Cuts missed payments and manual follow-up by school staff
Suggested

Cancellations & Refunds

  • Structured refund flow if a program is cancelled or a seat can't be honoured
  • Refund status visible in the receipt history
Suggested

Waitlist for Full Programs

  • Join a waitlist once seats run out, auto-notified if a spot opens
  • Prevents lost interest when a popular event fills up fast
Suggested

Ratings & Feedback on Programs

  • Quick rating after a program to help schools improve future ones
  • Builds trust in the global programs feed over time

Trust, Privacy & Compliance

India's DPDP Act treats a minor's data strictly — these are close to non-negotiable once real students are on the platform.

Recommended

Verifiable Parental Consent

  • DPDP-compliant consent flow before storing any student data
  • Required since accounts belong to minors, not just adults
Recommended

Data Rights Portal

  • Self-service screen to view, correct, or request deletion of data
  • Matches the "data principal rights" requirement under DPDP
Recommended

Role-Based Access & Audit Log

  • School staff only see data relevant to their role
  • Every access to student data is logged for accountability
Recommended

Fraud & Anomaly Detection

  • Flags duplicate payments or unusual receipt-redemption patterns
  • Protects both the school and the paying parent

Platform-Level Additions

Needed once the platform runs many schools at once, not just one pilot.

Later phase

Super Admin Panel

  • Platform team onboards new schools and manages their workspaces
  • Cross-school analytics without exposing schools to each other
Later phase

Sales & Demand Analytics

  • Helps a school forecast how many books/kits to stock next term
  • Shows platform team growth across schools and regions
Later phase

Offline-Friendly / Low-Data Mode

  • Lightweight PWA mode for patchy rural connectivity
  • Receipt still viewable offline once downloaded once
Later phase

Verified-School Badge

  • Visible trust marker once a school is verified by the platform team
  • Reassures parents paying an unfamiliar school for the first time
Page 04 Technical approach

Tech stack

A modern, decoupled architecture: a fast Next.js frontend that talks to a Headless CMS through an API layer. This is the approach most production apps of this size use today, rather than picking one monolithic framework.

Frontend

Next.js

Renders every screen — parent app and school console. Server-side rendering keeps it fast on low-end phones and weak networks.

API layer

REST API / GraphQL

The contract between the frontend and the content layer — handles catalogues, programs, orders and receipts.

Content layer

Headless CMS

Where schools' catalogues and programs actually live — editable without touching code.

StrapiSanityContentfulDirectusPayload CMS

Why this combination

Not choosing one or the other — using both together, the way most modern apps are built.

1

Fast frontend

Next.js gives quick page loads and a smooth mobile experience for parents.

2

Easy content editing

School staff update book lists and programs through the CMS — no developer needed.

3

Separation of concerns

Design and logic stay independent from content, so either can change without breaking the other.

4

Easier scaling

Onboarding a new school's workspace is a content change, not a code change.
